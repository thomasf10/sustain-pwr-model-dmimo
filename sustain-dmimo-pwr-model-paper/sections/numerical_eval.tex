\section{Numerical Evaluation}
\label{sec:numerical}

\subsection{Parameters}
\label{ssec:params}

The evaluation needs three groups of parameters. The deployment, channel and
frame of \Cref{sec:sysmodel} are collected in \Cref{tab:params_sys}. The
hardware constants of the \gls{ap} and of the co-located baseline are inherited
unchanged from the \gls{fr3} \gls{bs} model of \cite{peschiera2026fr3} and are
listed in \Cref{tab:params_hw}, so that the two deployments cannot differ in a
hardware constant. The quantities that exist only in a distributed network, that
is the fronthaul, the central unit and the per-\gls{ap} synchronization, are
collected in \Cref{tab:params_dmimo}.

\todo{Parameters that still need a decision rather than a source.
(i) The headline topology: \Cref{rem:fh_ceiling} and \Cref{rem:fpga} quote
$L=16$, $M=4$, whereas the rate model is configured with $L=40$, $M=4$, $K=20$;
one of the two has to move.
(ii) The frame in \Cref{tab:params_sys} gives $\tau_{\mathrm{UL,sig}}=1$, so the
uplink phase carries nothing but pilots and $R_{\mathrm{UL}}=0$. The evaluation
is then downlink-only and \eqref{eq:ee_net} reduces to
$R_{\mathrm{DL}}/P_{\mathrm{net}}$; either say so explicitly or give the uplink
a data phase.
(iii) With $\tau_{\mathrm{DL,sig}}=0$ the amplifier signalling level of
\eqref{eq:pa_avg_ap} is never reached, so $\zeta_{\mathrm{sig}}$ has no effect
on the reported numbers.
(iv) $\upsilon_{\mathrm{coh}}$ is stated outright as $200$ samples; the
Doppler-derived value of \cite{peschiera2026fr3}, at a maximum path-length
difference of \SI{300}{\metre} and a receiver velocity of
\SI{30}{\metre\per\second}, would be about $714$ samples at
$f_{c}=\SI{7}{\giga\hertz}$. Pick one and state which.
(v) $Q=64$ subcarriers are evaluated while the \gls{ofdm} system carries
$Q_{\mathrm{IFFT}}=4096$; say that the former is a Monte Carlo subset so the two
are not read as the same quantity.
(vi) \eqref{eq:pmimo} charges $P_{s}=\SI{1}{\watt}$ per \gls{fpga} to the
precoder, the combiner and the baseband filter, while the \gls{ifft} and
predistortion blocks each carry a flat \SI{0.1}{\watt} that does not depend on
the antenna count. \Cref{rem:fpga} discusses only the first, but both are paid
once per node: the network pays $L\times\SI{0.2}{\watt}$ of \gls{ifft} and
predistortion static where the co-located site pays \SI{0.2}{\watt}, an exactly
$L$-fold penalty, against the $L\lceil M/32\rceil$ versus $\lceil LM/32\rceil$
ratio of the \gls{fpga} term. The remark therefore understates the loss of
amortisation; either extend it or say that it treats the dominant term only.}
